## Reproduced defect and fix

S10's live validator rejected any fragment below 300 frames before reaching
its existing "short fragment waits for normal seal" path. A legal short final
fragment therefore became permanent `invalid`. The second capture also
exposes the claim/seal race: the worker's immutable claim snapshot predates
seal, even though seal is accepted by the time validation finishes.

The fix validates every rendition's codec, frame count/rate, timestamp bounds,
continuity and alignment, then treats a valid unsealed short fragment as
pending. A fresh sealed claim accepts it only at the inventory's actual final
sequence. A sealed short interior fragment remains invalid, including at the
durable commit boundary. No unsealed short tail advances the public pointer.
No frame-rate, inventory, digest, cutoff, membership or wire rule is relaxed.
New diagnostics record frame counts, media bounds and whether the claim was
sealed, without object URLs or credentials.

Regression coverage uses real 29.97 fps three-rendition short media and
PostgreSQL: before-seal waiting, seal after claim, successful fresh-claim tail,
and rejection of sealed short interior fragments. Corrupt sibling renditions
still fail rather than being hidden by another rendition's pending short tail.

poll cadence; claim leases and the two global processing slots still fence
concurrent work. Ended/expired/terminal/stale/backoff filters remain. No new
service, schedule, instance setting or event bus is added. A waiting execution
can retain its existing 4 vCPU / 8 GiB allocation for up to 50 additional
seconds; actual billed cost and live handover improvement remain to be measured.
The deadline bounds waiting/admission, not cancellation of a claim already run.
